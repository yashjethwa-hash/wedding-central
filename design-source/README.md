# Original artwork

The wordmarks exactly as exported, before processing: cream lettering on an
opaque white ground, each centred in a 1414×2000 canvas.

They are kept here, outside `public/`, so they are not served by the app. The
files in `public/` were derived from these by cropping to the glyphs and keying
the white ground out to transparency. Re-run that if you re-export: the white
ground has to become alpha, or the words render as white rectangles over the
damask background.
